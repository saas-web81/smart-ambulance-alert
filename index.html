HOW IT WORKS

From ambulance GPS to an open road.

A simple chain of connected decisions turns an emergency route into an early warning corridor.

01

Locate

The ambulance transmits its current location and destination to the control platform.

02

Analyze

The system evaluates the planned route and current traffic conditions.

03

Warn

Signals on the route activate before the ambulance reaches each road segment.

04

Clear

Drivers receive visual and acoustic warnings and can prepare to make way.

SYSTEM TECHNOLOGY

Built for smart-city emergency response.

AI

AI Route Control

Route-aware signal activation designed to anticipate the ambulance instead of waiting for it to arrive.

LED

Visual Alerts

High-visibility warning boards communicate the approaching emergency vehicle to road users.

🔊

Acoustic Alerts

Sound warnings provide an additional channel for drivers who may not immediately see the signal.

IoT

Connected Infrastructure

Roadside units and the central platform exchange status information in real time.
